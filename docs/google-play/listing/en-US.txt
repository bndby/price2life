=== App name (max 30) ===
Life to Price

=== Short description (max 80) ===
Convert a price into working time from your take-home income.

=== Full description (max 4000) ===
Life to Price turns an item price into working time, using the take-home income you enter.

Set your income and the period it covers: hour, day, week, month, or year. The app derives an hourly rate from a standard work schedule (8 hours a day, 40 hours a week, 2080 hours a year). Type a price and see the life time equivalent in years, months, days, and hours, plus the total working hours.

Optionally add your date of birth and sex. The app then estimates remaining life from WHO period life tables and shows life share: that working time as a percent of remaining life.

Numbers stay on this device. There is no account, and conversion does not require a network request.

Languages: English, Russian, and Simplified Chinese, or follow the device language.

The app does not connect to a bank, convert currencies, or store cents. Income and prices are whole numbers.
